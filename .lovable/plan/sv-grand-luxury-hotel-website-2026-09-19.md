# SV Grand Luxury Hotel Website

## Build
- Rebrand every visible reference and page title from Aranyam to **SV Grand**, with a refined hotel identity spanning dining, stays, experiences, reservations, and contact.
- Keep **Chef Narayana** as the only named chef and remove every reference to the previous chef.
- Preserve and upgrade the cinematic opening film so it appears only at the beginning of the main visit, includes a skip control, and transitions cleanly into the website.
- Expand the food offering into a substantial vegetarian and non-vegetarian collection with starters, mains, biryanis, breads, and desserts.
- Give every dish a clickable detail page with a large image, description, ingredients/highlights, price, dietary marker, related dishes, and reservation action.
- Add dedicated, fully designed pages for the menu, Chef Narayana, hotel experience, reservations, and contact/location; all navigation links and calls-to-action will lead somewhere meaningful.
- Keep reservations as a polished working request flow with clear confirmation; no database or payment system will be added.

## Experience and motion
- Retain the black, antique-gold, ivory, botanical-green, and wine-red visual language while making it feel more like a luxury hotel than a single restaurant.
- Add restrained depth through scroll reveals, image parallax, perspective hover, layered section movement, cursor-responsive photography, and smooth route transitions.
- Keep motion cinematic and slow rather than flashy, with touch-safe behavior and reduced-motion alternatives.
- Use varied editorial compositions, precise typography, strong photography, and deliberate spacing so pages feel individually art-directed rather than repeated from one template.

## Structure
- Home: opening film, hotel arrival, dining philosophy, signature dishes, Chef Narayana, stay/experience highlights, guest notes, and reservation prompt.
- Menu: filterable full collection with dedicated vegetarian and non-vegetarian groupings.
- Dish detail: individual page for each menu item, addressed through a stable shareable URL.
- Chef: Chef Narayana’s story, culinary point of view, and signature dishes.
- Experience: dining room, hospitality, private celebrations, and hotel atmosphere.
- Reserve: focused reservation request and confirmation.
- Contact: location, hours, phone/email, and clear enquiry actions.

## Technical details
- Use TanStack Router route files for each main page and one validated dynamic dish route for individual menu items.
- Centralize hotel, navigation, and dish content so names, prices, links, and related recommendations stay consistent everywhere.
- Build shared navigation, footer, transition, reveal, and dish components while giving each page a distinct composition.
- Reuse the existing original imagery and opening video, adding only media needed to avoid obvious repetition.
- Add unique title, description, Open Graph title/description, Open Graph type, and Twitter card metadata to every content route.
- Verify all links, dish filters, dish pages, reservation flow, intro behavior, and desktop/mobile presentation in the live preview.
